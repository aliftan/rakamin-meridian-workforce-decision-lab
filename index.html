Decision: validate demand before expansion.

Without validated receiving work, the model does not support a full-absorption promise. Non-gateable rollout makes the timing risk more immediate.

Decision rule: Selective gating is worth evaluating only where verified receiving work exists, extra months improve assessed readiness, and Operations/Technology can authorize the timing. The illustrative Rp600m is foregone economic benefit—not automatically a cash payment.

Calculation boundary: required work = (3,570 realized displacement − 225 relevant attrition)/0.65 = 5,146 at M18. This is a target to discover/validate, not open positions. Scenario curves are model-anchored interpolations, not a live recalculation of all 36 monthly spreadsheet drivers.

Gating shifts timing. In this demonstration the two rollout modes converge by approximately M20; gating does not create permanent new roles or reduce the ultimate gap. The curve uses anchors from the earlier synthetic model and interpolation between anchors, not a production forecasting engine.

Evidence promotion rules

01 · CLAIMED

Someone expects the need

Stakeholder statement; not enough to promise placements.

02 · BACKED

Workload, budget and timing tested

Two materially independent sources, operating owner and funded/authorized capacity.

03 · MATCHABLE

Human feasibility checked

Capability, time-to-competence, location, legal authority, safety, consent and staffing constraints.

04 · CONFIRMED PLACEMENT

Separate observed outcome

Recorded only after an actual authorised human decision and transition.

Within 30 days: Operations supplies sampled task-hours and service workload; Finance/Procurement validates contractor contract expiry, avoidability and vendor spend. When evidence fails, keep demand at “claimed”—do not manufacture absorption capacity.

LEAST-CONFIDENT DECISION

Demand-led redeployment as the primary strategic bet

We are least confident in choosing demand-led redeployment as the central intervention because sufficient receiving work has not been proven. Test this decision within 30 days using operational workload evidence from Operations and avoidable contractor spend from Finance. If the evidence fails, adjust the programme toward controlled interim capacity management, task redesign and Board-funded transition measures—not a promise of full absorption.

Economics boundaries: avoided external recruitment and overtime are currently Rp0 modelled, pending Finance data; this does not imply their real value is zero. Gateable NPV is withheld until month-specific gated cash flows are established. Productive non-contractor value is not cash ROI.

Institutionalise without erasing identity

Repeatability
