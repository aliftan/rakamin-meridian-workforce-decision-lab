Investment scope & decision gates

Cost bridge · Rp million

Rp6,000 = Rp3,500 consulting fee (brief) + Rp1,500 implementation + Rp500 support in each of years 2 and 3 (synthetic). At Rp6m per genuinely avoidable vendor FTE-month, break-even is 1,000 FTE-months; at the full hypothetical 3,900 volume, the unit hurdle is Rp1.54m. The Rp3,787.5m architecture estimate is a separate resource-effort valuation, with overlap not yet reconciled—not an automatic extra bill.

Decision permission

Day 30: establish union safeguards and request Operations/Finance/Technology data. Day 60: proceed to a bounded opt-in pilot only where at least one receiving role is Backed; otherwise no scaled matching claim. Day 90: four evidence, operational, decision and trust gates determine continue / adjust / hold. M1–3 spending allocation of Rp1,250m is illustrative: Rp875m pro-rated existing fee and Rp375m unapproved implementation allowance.

Unpriced or not reconciled: licences, travel, employee time, full reskilling and mobility. The contractor option is absent from the brief. All costs and role capacities require Client validation.

Economics boundaries: avoided external recruitment and overtime are currently Rp0 modelled, pending Finance data; this does not imply their real value is zero. Gateable NPV is withheld until month-specific gated cash flows are established. Productive non-contractor value is not cash ROI.
