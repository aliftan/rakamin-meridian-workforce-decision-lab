EXECUTIVE DECISION
Choose a demand-led capability transformation.
The first investment is a proof of decision quality, not a promise to absorb every displaced work equivalent.
WHAT WE BUILD
A capability layer above positions: work redesign → task bands → evidence portfolio → human-reviewed transition.
WHERE WE PROVE IT
Field Metering after the Day-30 discovery. Test smart-meter-adjacent demand in 1–2 regions with volunteer safeguards.
WHAT WE DO NOT PROMISE
No mass layoffs; no system replacement; no enterprise-wide individual scoring; no unverified absorption target.
Board ask: authorize a cross-functional evidence sprint, controlled pilot and decision rights; release scale only through four gates.
BOARD OUTCOMES / MEASURES & DECISION RIGHTS
What the Board can hold us to.
The first 90 days establish verified decisions—not a fabricated placement target. Track people, receiving-work capacity and work equivalents separately.
